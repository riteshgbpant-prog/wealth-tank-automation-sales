## Scripts (`scripts/`)

- `engine.py`: canvas, captions, name plates, transitions (flash, whip, wipe, zoom, glitch, iris, panels, spin, light leak)
- `teaser.py`: cinematic look (grade, bars, flares, shake), cold open, photo bursts, slide slams, countdown, designation tags
- `cards.py`: invitation, fact punches, modules card, thank-you and outro cards
- `anurag_teaser.py`, `ritesh_teaser.py`, `reel_anurag.py`, `reel_ritesh.py`: timelines for the four videos
- `anthem.py`, `music.py`, `instruments.py`: original dhol/brass/choir score and sound effects synced to each timeline
- `outros.py`: end screens with phone numbers
